# Idea 2: Smart Waste Bin with Fill-Level and Odour Prediction

> **Proposed by:** Wern She  
> A bin that detects its fill level, identifies waste conditions and predicts when it needs to be collected.

| Component | Details |
|---|---|
| **ESP32 #1** | Ultrasonic sensor for fill level |
| **ESP32 #2** | Gas, temperature, humidity and weight sensors |
| **Edge action (real-time)** | Show a warning or operate a lid mechanism |
| **ML** | Predict when the bin will overflow, or classify normal vs abnormal odour conditions |
| **Cloud (long-term)** | Collection trends and estimated next collection time |
| **Demo** | Add objects to the bin and simulate different waste conditions |
| **Stretch** | Camera to classify recyclable vs non-recyclable waste (not required for the project to work) |

## Notes against the brief

- Cheap and low-risk hardware; mostly kit sensors.
- Smart rubbish bins are on the course's list of past projects, so we would need a clear twist to score on novelty.
- Overlaps with idea 4 and could be merged with it (see below).
